Open jobs

Drive to one of these signs. Each gives its own kind of job, as often as you like.

  • Supply run: a heavy load, slow uphill. More pay for a car without a new dent.
  • Rush job: a short clock. Every second left over pays three times.
  • Clean run: pays well. Break the law on the way and the job is gone.
The arrow points to the nearest. All of them are on the map: tap the little map.

Drive any city.

The real streets and buildings of any place on Earth, seen from above. Walk up to a car and take it, run deliveries against the clock for cash (three make a run, for the scoreboard or to send to a friend to beat), and shake off the police when you've been careless.

This page carries the map of central Berlin, about 3 km across. The full game loads any city.

There isn't much on the map here yet. You can play, but the streets will feel empty. We'd recommend adding to the map first: this game is built from OpenStreetMap, which anyone can edit.

Touch
Drag anywhere on the left to walk or drive that way. Buttons on the right: in / out, brake. Tap the little map for a big one of the streets around you.
